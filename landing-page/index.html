How Skavenge Works

A cryptographically secure scavenger hunt where every clue is an NFT you truly own

1

Mint Your Clue

Create encrypted clues as ERC-721 NFTs. Set point values, optional ETH rewards, and sale prices. Your clue content is encrypted using ElGamal encryption, ensuring only the owner can read it.

2

Solve & Earn

Submit your solution to the smart contract. If correct, you're awarded points and any attached ETH reward instantly. Solved clues are permanently marked, preventing gaming of the system.

3

Trade Securely

List unsolved clues for sale or buy from others. Our cryptographic proof system ensures fair trades, the seller can't cheat by providing fake encrypted content, and neither party can scam the other.

4

Win Prizes

Accumulate points by solving clues to increase your chances of winning the grand prize. The more clues you solve, the better your odds. Compete with players worldwide!

Why Skavenge?

Built for security, transparency, and true ownership

🔒

Encrypted Content

ElGamal encryption on secp256k1 ensures only NFT owners can decrypt and read clue content. Your secrets stay secret.

🔗

Trustless Trading

DLEQ cryptographic proofs verify trades mathematically. No trust required, the protocol guarantees fair exchanges.

💰

True Ownership

Every clue is a real ERC-721 NFT. You own it, control it, and can trade it freely on any compatible marketplace.

🚀

Instant Rewards

Solve a clue and receive ETH rewards immediately. No waiting, no intermediaries, just direct blockchain transfers.

🔍

On-Chain Verification

Solutions are verified by the smart contract through hash matching. Transparent, auditable, and tamper-proof.

🌐

Decentralized

All game logic runs on Ethereum. No central authority can change the rules or manipulate outcomes.
